Sorting Desk

For each bookmark, click the topic it belongs in. If you don't want it, click Don't Keep. If it's worth keeping but no topic fits, skip it (Scatha may find a new topic for it later). If you're torn, click Not Sure (a short note helps). Only your clicks count: if you skip a bookmark, Scatha won't assume you agree with Jev. Each click is saved right away. When you press Done, Scatha will write full notes for the bookmarks you sorted out of Unsorted. (It will ask you first, in the terminal.)
